---
title: "Clients"
description: "A customer of yours. Assign a worker to a client and that client gets its own copy, with its own memory and nothing of any other client's."
---

A **client** is a company you work for. On its own, a client in Ergoly is a record: a name, a website, a logo. It starts doing something when you assign a worker to it. From then on there is a copy of that worker for that client, and every channel and person you link to the client reaches that copy.

The **Clients** page lists them with tabs for Active, Inactive and Archived, and each client's page has four tabs: **Overview** (the About text, details, and Channels & People), **Workers**, **Tasks**, and **Connections**.
