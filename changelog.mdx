<Update label="November 9, 2026" description="Artifacts on your own address, and workers that answer to their name" rss={{ title:"Artifacts on your own address, and workers that answer to their name",description:"Share artifacts from your own web address, protect them with a password, and talk to your workers by name. Plus faster failure notices, workers that recover on their own, and fixes for slow tools, large Slack workspaces and scheduled task reporting.",date:"2026-11-09" }}>
  This week your shared artifacts can carry your name instead of ours, your workers are easier to talk to, and a worker that breaks mid-reply tells you sooner.

  ## Your own address for artifacts

  Artifacts you share can use your own web address, so the people you send them to never see an Ergoly link.

  ## Passwords on artifacts

  Artifacts on your own address can be password protected, with a different password for each artifact. You can send one artifact to one customer and another to someone else.

  ## Talk to a worker by name

  Start a message with a worker's name and it answers, with no @ needed. This works at the top of a channel and in a thread, and also when the name opens a question, such as "Wicket, are you there?".

  ## "Is thinking…" in Slack

  When you message a worker, Slack shows that it is thinking until its first update or answer.

  ## Keep up with a room

  Ask a worker to keep an eye on a channel and it sets up a recurring catch-up that reads the room and remembers what matters. It posts nothing unless you ask.

  ## Improvements

  - **Faster notice when a reply breaks midway.** When a worker's reply breaks partway through, the person who wrote to it is now told within about two minutes, instead of about fourteen.
  - **Workers recover on their own.** A worker whose workspace breaks now comes back on its own the next time someone messages it.
  - **Rooms no longer answer everything.** A room can no longer be set to answer every message, and the listen setting is gone. Existing rooms follow the new behavior.
  - **Settings is for company admins.** The Settings page in control is now for company admins only.

  ## Fixes

  - **Slow tools get time to answer.** Tools connected to a worker can now take up to five minutes to answer, up from one minute, so slow tools return their result instead of losing it.
  - **More than five accounts of one app.** A worker can keep six or more accounts of the same app connected without losing connections.
  - **Complete channel lists.** Channel lists in large Slack workspaces are no longer cut short, so workers and the channel picker see every channel.
  - **Task run times.** Reading your tasks from outside Ergoly now shows when each task last ran and when it runs next.
  - **Failed task messages.** When a scheduled task's direct message cannot be sent, the run now shows as failed and keeps Slack's reason, so it can be fixed.
