Each worker has an email address under a sending domain Ergoly sets up for your company when your first worker is built. It looks like `nora@youragency.ergoly-mail.com`. A client's copy has its own, with the client's name in it, so mail about Acme comes from Acme's copy. Mail goes out under the worker's name.

## Sending {#sending}

Ask the worker to email someone and it does, with to, cc and bcc, attachments up to about 3 MB, and replies into an existing thread. It sends at most five emails a minute and to at most a hundred recipients at once. Every email it sent is listed on the worker's **Settings** tab under **Email**, with when, from, subject and status.

## Switching it off {#off}

The same card has a kill switch: **Switch email off** stops the worker sending anything until you switch it back on.

## The domain {#domain}

Under **Settings → Email** for the company you can see the domain's status, the records Ergoly published for it, and **Re-check now**. You do not set anything up yourself.
