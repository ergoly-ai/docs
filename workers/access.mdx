A worker's access is three lists: the **clients** it serves, the **channels** it answers in, and the **people** whose direct messages belong to a client. All three are on the worker's **Clients** tab, in one table with the caption _Where messages to this Worker go: a client, or your own team (internal)_.

## Adding a client {#add}

Press **Add to client**. Pick the **Client**, then tick **Channels** and **People**. As the dialog says, _channels route by conversation, people route their DMs and group DMs_. A channel or person already linked to another client is greyed out: nothing is moved silently. See [Assigning a worker](/clients/assigning-a-worker).

## Channels {#channels}

Each channel row can be **Mark internal**, **Reassign** to another client, or removed. **Add channel** lists channels the worker can see that are not set up yet: public ones it can **Join**, private ones you invite it to first with `/invite`. Details under [Channels](/workers/channels).

<Note>
  **Worth knowing.** A worker marked internal-only has no Add to client button. Its tab says: _This is an internal-only worker: it can never be put in front of a client. It works in DMs and internal channels only._ Your AI Admin is one.
</Note>
