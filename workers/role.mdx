title: "Role"
description: "The job title, and the one-line job it does. A worker is a role, not an automation."
---

A worker's **role** is its job title and what the job is for: *Account Manager*, *SEO Analyst*, *Bookkeeper*. You set the title when you create the worker and can change it under Settings → Identity. The title is what people see under its name, and it is the first thing the worker reads about itself.

## One worker, one role {#one-role}

The question of how many workers to run is a question about roles, not tasks. Divide by role: if two jobs would be done by two different people at your agency, judged by two different standards, make two workers. If one person would do both, make one worker and give it two skills. [Staff it, don't wire it](https://www.ergoly.ai/thoughts/staff-it-dont-wire-it) makes the full argument.

## Persona {#persona}

From the name, the title and the company, Ergoly writes the worker's persona: who it is, how it introduces itself, whose name it works under. The persona is rewritten whenever the worker is rebuilt, so it always matches what control says. The parts you own are the [responsibilities](/workers/responsibilities) and [behaviors](/workers/behaviors).

<Note>
  **Worth knowing.** Give the title the way you would on a job posting. A worker with a vague title asks more questions than one with a clear one.
</Note>
