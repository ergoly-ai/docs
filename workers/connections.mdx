A worker uses whatever **connections** reach it: apps your company has connected and placed with the company, with this worker, with a client, or with this worker for one client. The worker's **Connections** tab lists the apps that reach it in the chosen context, and nothing else.

It never holds a password or a key. It calls the app by name, and Ergoly supplies the credential on the way out. Connect and place apps on the company's **Connections** page; the worker's tab is a view of the result.

## From Slack {#slack}

A worker can also ask for a connection itself, within its own reach. For an app that signs in, it posts a link anyone in the conversation can open; for an app that takes a key, you paste the key in the thread and it goes straight to the vault, never echoed back. A client's copy can place a connection only with itself for that client, or with that client.
