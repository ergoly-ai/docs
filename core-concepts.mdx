title: "Core concepts"

## [Company](/company)

Your agency on Ergoly. The organization chart of who works here—people and workers, clients and vendors.

### [Clients](/clients)

A customer of yours. Assign a worker, and that client gets its own copy automatically.

## [Workers](/workers)

AI entities at your company with a role, responsibilities, and behaviors you set. Performs work for you and your clients.

### [Knowledge](/workers/knowledge)

What a worker remembers and the skills it can run.

### Channels

Where a worker operates: Slack channels and DMs, email, and the web.

## Work

Tasks and routines, the tools a worker is connected to, approvals, and the pages it publishes.
