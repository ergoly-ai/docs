---
title: "Core Concepts"
---

# Company

Your agency on Ergoly. The chart of who works here, people and workers alike.

# Worker

An AI member of your company with a role, responsibilities, and behaviors you set.

# Client

A customer of yours. Assign a worker, and that client gets its own copy.

# Channel

Where a worker operates: Slack channels and DMs, email, and the web.

# Knowledge

What a worker remembers and the skills it can run.

# Work

Tasks and routines, the tools a worker is connected to, approvals, and the pages it publishes.
