## Ask your AI Admin {#admin}

Tell it the client's name and, if you have it, the website. It confirms what it is about to create and does it on your yes.

## In control {#control}

1. Open **Clients** and press **New Client**, or open **Company**, press **Add**, and choose **Client**.
2. Type the **Company name**. The slug under it is the client's short name in URLs; click it to change it.
3. Add the **Website URL** if you have it. Ergoly uses it for the logo and a public description.
4. Optionally paste a **Slack Channel ID** for the channel you share with the client. You can do this later from the worker instead.
5. Press **Create client**.

## From a channel {#channel}

When a worker joins a channel and no client matches, it offers **New client…**. That creates the client and maps the channel to it in one step. See [Slack](/workers/channels/slack).

<Note>
  **Worth knowing.** A client with no worker assigned does nothing. The next step is [assigning a worker](/clients/assigning-a-worker).
</Note>
