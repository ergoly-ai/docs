Before a worker talks to Acme directly, give it a channel where your team can see what it would say. That channel is a client channel, even though it's internal facing, because only your people are in it, but the work in it is about one client.

## Setting it up {#setup}

1. Create the channel in Slack with your own people in it.
2. Invite the worker. When it asks what the channel is for, press **Client** channel, not internal.
3. Ask the worker for the client's work there: *draft Acme's weekly status*. That client's copy of the worker answers, with Acme's knowledge folder and connections in reach, so the draft is about Acme.
4. When the drafts are right, invite the worker to the client-facing channel Acme is in, and set that one up for Acme.
