title: "Ergoly"
description: "AI Workers you own, separated per client, inside the Slack your agency already uses."
---

Ergoly gives your agency **workers**: AI members of your Slack workspace, each with a name, a job, and memory of its own. You teach a worker how your agency does a piece of work. It does that work for your clients, in their channels, under your name.

Every client you assign a worker to gets a separate copy of that worker. The copy for Acme knows Acme and nothing about Globex. What a worker learns about the job travels to every client; what it learns about a client stays with that client.

## The shape of it {#shape}

| Thing | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Company | Your agency on Ergoly. The chart of who works here, people and workers alike. |
| Worker | An AI member of your company with a role, responsibilities, and behaviors you set. |
| Client | A customer of yours. Assign a worker, and that client gets its own copy. |
| Channel | Where a worker operates: Slack channels and DMs, email, and the web. |
| Knowledge | What a worker remembers and the skills it can run. |
| Work | Tasks and routines, the tools a worker is connected to, approvals, and the pages it publishes. |
