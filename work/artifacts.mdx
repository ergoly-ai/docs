## Creating one {#creating}

Ask the worker for anything you would rather pass around as a link: a report, a dashboard, a chart, a comparison, a one-pager, a calculator. It builds a self-contained page, publishes it, and replies with the link and whether it is private or public. Ask for changes and a new version appears at the same link.

## Publishing and locations {#publishing}

Every artifact has a **Location**: your company, or one client. The location decides which workers can find and read it: all of them, or only that client's. A new artifact's location follows the worker that made it. Any signed-in member of your company can open every artifact, whatever its location. The **Artifacts** page lists them all, with filters for author, location and sharing.

## Privacy and sharing {#permissions}

On the artifact's page, the **Sharing** popover offers **Private** (people in your company) or **Public** (anyone with the link, no sign-in). New artifacts are private. There is no delete: setting an artifact back to private is how you take it down.

<Note>
  **Worth knowing.** A page can hold a lot: up to 4.5 MB, with charts and tables. It cannot hold live data, forms, or anything that stores what a reader types.
</Note>
