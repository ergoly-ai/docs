Your **AI Admin** is a worker that only works inside your company. It never joins a client's channel. Its job is to set things up: workers, channels, clients, and the tools they use.

## Setup, in control {#setup}

Setup has four steps.

1. **Connect Slack.** Ergoly needs permission to create Slack apps in your workspace. On api.slack.com/apps, open **App Configuration Tokens**, press **Generate Token**, and paste the token that starts with `xoxe-` into the **Refresh token** field.
2. **Name your AI Admin.** Type a name (the example is Ada). Leave **Full Slack permissions** on unless you have a reason not to; it lets the AI Admin invite workers to channels and create channels for you.
3. **Install it.** Press **Add &lt;name> to Slack** and click **Allow** in the Slack window that opens. Control shows *Starting &lt;name> up…* while the worker is built.
4. **Done.** Control shows *&lt;name> is looking around your workspace*, then *&lt;name> has messaged you in Slack*.

## Then, in Slack {#slack}

Open the direct message from your AI Admin. It introduces itself and asks what the first worker should do. Answer in plain words. It proposes a name, a job, and the channels, and asks for your yes before creating anything.

<Note>
  **Worth knowing.** Only an admin can create the AI Admin, because it holds a key to your whole company. A member who opens setup sees *An admin sets up your AI Admin*. If your company already has workers, the link to add one is above the Company page's List tab.
</Note>
