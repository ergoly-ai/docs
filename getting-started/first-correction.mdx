A worker learns the way a new hire does: by being told. You do not configure it. You correct it in the thread where the work happened, and it keeps the rule.

## Where the rule goes {#where}

When a worker learns something, it asks itself who the thing is true for. A client's preference is filed with that client. A better way of doing the job is filed with the worker, so every client benefits. A rule for the whole agency is filed with the company. If it is not sure, it files narrower, and tells you where it put it.

## Checking what it kept {#check}

Everything a worker remembers is a file you can read. Open the worker in control, choose the **Knowledge** tab, and pick the client from the context picker at the top. The note is there, in plain Markdown, and you can edit it. See [Memory](/workers/knowledge/memory).

<Note>
  **Worth knowing.** A correction about how the worker behaves in general, rather than a fact about a client, belongs in its behaviors. The worker proposes the exact line and adds it only after your explicit yes. See [Behaviors](/workers/behaviors).
</Note>
