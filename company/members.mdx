Two pages under Company list who works here.

- **People**: One row per person from your Slack workspace who is on the chart, is a client's contact, or is waiting in the tray. Columns: Name, Team, Reports to. Nobody is invited here; people appear once a worker is installed in your workspace, and you place them.
- **Workers**: One row per worker, with its team, the clients it serves, and its status. **New Worker** starts the create wizard. A row opens the worker's own page.

## Who can sign in {#signin}

Being on the chart does not give someone a login. Logins to control are managed under **Settings**, on the **Members** tab, where you invite people by email and set their role. An **admin** can invite people, create API keys, and set up the AI Admin. A **member** can do everything else.

## Worker statuses {#statuses}

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Active | Installed and answering. |
| Onboarding · Provisioning | Being built. Give it a few minutes. |
| Onboarding · Awaiting install | Waiting for a Slack admin to click Allow. |
| Active · Stopped | Paused from the worker's Settings tab. |
| Failed / Error | Something went wrong during setup. Open the worker for details and a Retry. |
| Quarantined | Ergoly stopped it after repeated failures. The worker's page says why and has an Unquarantine button. |
