This week you have more control over the workers you use, a wish carries to every worker that serves you, and what you spend is easier to see.

## Stop a worker, and set its recurring tasks

Anyone who can use a worker can now stop it and change its recurring tasks. Deleting a worker is limited to your organization's admins, and no one is given edit rights wider than the rights they have to use it.

## A wish, said once

Tell a worker how you like to be answered — start long replies with a summary, no bullet points — and every worker in your organization follows it for you, and for you only.

## Usage and cost you can see

The Usage page now shows what each turn cost at verified rates, and lets you pull a summary over any date range.

## A word before your worker hours run out

Your organization's admins are told in Slack when worker hours reach 80%, 90% and 100%.

## Invite people from the People page

An organization admin can invite someone into the organization from the People page. The dialog confirms the invitation and closes, and the first time someone signs in they are asked for their name and picture.

## Improvements

- **Large tool results are kept, not cut.** When a tool returns more than a worker can show, the whole result is kept in a file rather than truncated.
- **A recurring task that finds nothing new starts no turn.** A recurring task whose check finds nothing new no longer starts a turn, so watching something quiet costs nothing.
- **A paused organization stays quiet.** A paused organization's scheduled tasks, webhooks and platform turns are skipped while it is paused, rather than kept to run later.
