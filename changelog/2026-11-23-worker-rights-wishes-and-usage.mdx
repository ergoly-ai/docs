This week what you can do with a worker follows what you can already do with it, a wish you state once sticks across every worker in your organization, and a question put to a room gets answered by the worker whose job it is.

## More of what you can do with a worker you use

If you can use a worker, you can now stop it and set its recurring tasks; only a company admin can delete it. Change access always includes use.

## State a wish once

Tell a worker how you like to be answered, and every worker in your organization follows it for you and no one else. It carries across workers without your repeating it.

## Ask a room and the right worker answers

Put a request to a channel that holds several workers, naming none, and the worker whose job it is answers. A message that mentions a person or another app is left to them.

## Usage and cost you can see, as it happens

Your Usage page shows the cost of each turn at verified rates and totals over a date range, and your admins are told in Slack as your workers' hours reach 80%, 90% and 100%.

## Improvements

- **Inviting people is smoother** — A company admin can invite a person into the organization from the People page, the invite dialog confirms and closes itself, and a person signing in for the first time is asked for their name and picture.
- **Large results are kept, not cut** — When a tool returns more than a worker can show, the whole result is kept in a file the worker can still use, instead of being trimmed.
- **A recurring task with nothing new doesn't spend a turn** — A recurring task whose check finds nothing new starts no run, so a quiet check no longer uses your worker hours.

## Fixes

- **No duplicate answers** — A worker no longer posts the same answer twice in a thread from a single turn.
- **One answer per turn, and working notes stay out of the room** — A worker shows brief progress as it works and posts a single answer; its working notes no longer reach the channel.
- **A reply in a thread you started reaches the worker** — A reply in a thread a worker started is now treated as a follow-up, the same as a thread it replied in.
