A smaller week: external companies can now appear on your org chart, and a task that fails at startup no longer gets stuck.

## Vendors on the org chart

External companies can now be added to the Company org chart alongside your workers and people. Each vendor carries a logo, a website, a short description, a lead and a list of members, and can be shown or hidden per operator.

## Improvements

- **Ongoing security hardening.** We shipped a round of security fixes and hardening measures.

## Fixes

- **Stuck task runs.** A task that failed before it could start used to sit in a pending state indefinitely. It now settles as failed, posts a notice in the run's thread and clears.
