Email sending is live, and workers are substantially more reliable at reading the conversation they're in, finding their own skills and knowing who's in the room.

## Worker email

Every worker now has its own address and can send email directly. The control panel shows your organization's sending domain, its DNS records, each worker's address and an on/off switch, and every message sent is logged.

## Workers know what they know

Each turn, a worker sees an index of everything in its memory, with a description for every file. It can check what it knows before saying it doesn't — fewer invented or inconsistent answers.

## Better at Slack

Workers look up who's in a channel, who a person is and what was said earlier directly, without a detour. They also report progress mid-turn without sending a reply, and escalations reliably reach a person.

## Scheduled tasks can do what a live turn can

A scheduled run can now post into a thread, a DM or a new message — anything a worker can do when you're talking to it.

## Fixes

- **Thread context.** Workers couldn't always read the thread they were replying in. They now have the full thread on every turn.
- **Knowing itself.** Workers sometimes read their own @mention as someone else's and mixed up people in the room. They now know their own identity.
- **Channel setup menu.** The menu shown when a worker first joins a channel wasn't appearing. It's back.
- **Connections and new-worker setup.** Both pages could fail to load with an error. Fixed.
- **Switching organizations.** Switching organization from the sidebar led to a missing page. It now lands where it should.
- **Company page scrolling.** A wide org chart scrolled the whole page sideways. The chart now pans and zooms inside its own frame.
- **Silent Slack failures.** When Slack rejected a reply, nobody found out. The worker now posts a notice that something went wrong.
