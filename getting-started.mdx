title: "Getting started"
description: "From the invitation to your first worker answering in Slack."
---

Ergoly takes on a few agencies at a time, and someone from Ergoly sets your company up with you. After that, your AI Admin takes over: a worker that lives in your Slack and builds the rest of your company from a conversation.

1. [Getting access](/getting-started/access): you accept an invitation to control and a shared Slack channel with the Ergoly team.
2. [Meet your AI Admin](/getting-started/ai-admin): you connect Slack, name your AI Admin, and install it. It messages you and sets up your first worker.
3. [Add a client and a channel](/getting-started/first-client): you tell the AI Admin who the client is and which channel is theirs.
4. [Your first correction](/getting-started/first-correction): you tell the worker what to do differently, once, and it keeps the rule.

<Note>
  **Worth knowing.** Everything your AI Admin does, you can also do in control. The pages under Company, Workers and Clients show both. The AI Admin asks before it changes anything, so nothing happens without your yes.
</Note>
